Addons & Settings

Recommended addons, handy macros and game settings for WoW: Forever. Members can add their own tips.

New to addons? Install the CurseForge app, choose the "Forever" version of World of Warcraft and start with everything marked Must-have (plus what fits your role/class). In-game: Esc → AddOns to turn them on/off, /reload after changes. Old Classic addons often don't work – always take the Forever version.
