Guild Bank – Opie Bank

Our shared stock of items and gold, kept on the bank character Opie Bank.

How it works

  • Got spare items or gold? Mail them in-game to Opie Bank (or put them in the guild bank), then add them to the list below so everyone can see what's in stock.
  • Picked it up? Lower the count here (or remove the line). Reservations on an item disappear with it.
  • Gold: we are saving 100 gold to buy a shared guild bank. Whoever updates the gold in-game, also updates it here.
  • Everyone who is logged in can add, change and remove items – keep it honest.
