Guild Bank – Opie Bank

Our shared stock of items and gold, kept on the bank character Opie Bank.

How it works

  • Got spare items or gold? Mail them in-game to Opie Bank. Jelle keeps the Opie Bank inventory up to date; you can see it below.
  • Sent something? Add it under Send to Opie Bank so Jelle knows it is coming. Once he has it, it moves into the inventory.
  • Picked it up? Lower the count here (or remove the line). Reservations on an item disappear with it.
  • Gold: we are saving 100 gold to buy a shared guild bank. Mail your gold for the guild to Opie Bank as well; Jelle then updates the amount here.
